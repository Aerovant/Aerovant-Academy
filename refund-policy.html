Aerovant Academy strives to deliver high-quality training and educational services.

1. Refund Eligibility

Refund requests may be considered if:

  • Requested within 7 days of purchase
  • Less than 20% of course content accessed

All refund requests must include the order details and reason for request.

2. Non-Refundable Cases

Refunds will not be issued if:

  • Course materials are fully accessed
  • The refund period has expired
  • The course was purchased during promotional discounts
  • Policy violations occur

3. Refund Processing

Approved refunds will be processed within 7–10 business days.

Refunds will be issued to the original payment method.

4. Course Cancellation

Aerovant Academy reserves the right to cancel or reschedule courses due to unforeseen circumstances.

In such cases, users may choose:

  • A replacement batch, or
  • Full refund

5. Refund Requests

Refund requests should be sent to: support@aerovantacademy.com

Include:

  • Order ID
  • Course name
  • Purchase date
  • Reason for refund

Cancellation Policy

Users may cancel course enrollment within 7 days of purchase, subject to the refund eligibility conditions mentioned above.

After course access has been substantially used, cancellations will not be accepted.

Disclaimer

All training and educational content provided by Aerovant Academy is intended for learning purposes only.

Aerovant Academy does not guarantee:

  • Job placement
  • Salary outcomes
  • Certification success

Results may vary depending on individual effort and industry conditions.
