Why we exist

Your degree shows you studied. Employers ask what you’ve built.

College teaches theory and tests it on paper. A fresher interview asks three other questions, and college rarely prepares you for any of them. The Academy exists to close that gap.

  • What have you built?
  • Can you explain how it works?
  • Can you solve a problem in front of us?

Everything we teach is built around those three.

Learn, practise, build, prepare.

Four stages, in this order. Every stage ends with something you can show.

  1. Learn.

    Live classes with a mentor. You get the core skills of your track, taught from the basics.

  2. Practise.

    A hands-on task with every topic, reviewed by your mentor. Your working code goes into your own GitHub.

  3. Build.

    One complete project, built by you and reviewed the way work is reviewed in a company.

  4. Prepare.

    Resume, GitHub clean-up and mock interviews, so you can explain your project under pressure.

What makes us different.

Six things we hold ourselves to. Each one is something you can check in a free demo class before you pay anything.

  • Practical projects

Our connection with Aerovant Technologies.

The Academy is not a separate institute that borrows a company’s name. It is part of the company, and the two share people and work.

The company
Aerovant Technologies builds software products and secures cloud systems. Visit aerovanttech.com
The Academy
The company’s training arm. It teaches college students through courses, internships with training and workshops.
Mentors
Mentors work on live products and security engagements, and bring that work into class.
Internships

After a course
