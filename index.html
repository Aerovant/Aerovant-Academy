    Questions students and parents ask us.

    Do I need a coding background?

    No. Every course starts from the basics. Software Testing and Data Analysis suit any degree. AI goes faster if you already know some Python.

    Is a job guaranteed?

    No. We prepare your resume, project and interviews, and refer you where we can. Getting hired depends on your work too.

    Can I do this alongside college?

    Yes. Everything here is built for college students. Classes run on weekday evenings / weekends / semester breaks.

    Online or classroom: which is better?

    Same mentor and same syllabus either way. Choose classroom if you live near Krishnagiri and learn better in a room.

    What do I need to bring?

    A laptop and a steady internet connection.

    Which language are classes in?

    English, with explanations in Tamil where it helps

    What is the fee, and can I pay in parts?

    ₹fee for the full four months. You can pay in n instalments.

    What certificate do I get?

    A course completion certificate from Aerovant Academy. It is not a university or government certification.

    Do I get paid for the internship?

    No. It is a training internship. You pay a fee that covers training, mentor review, project work and your certificate.

    Will my college accept the internship?

    You get an internship certificate from Aerovant Technologies and a project report. Send us your college’s format before you start, and we’ll follow it.

    How is a workshop different from a course?

    A workshop is one or two days on a single topic. A course is four months and ends with a project. Start with a workshop if you’re not sure.
